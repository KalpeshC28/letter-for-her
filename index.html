01 · where it all began

who would've
thought?

prachi, i was actually thinking about how random all of this is. we had already met in uttarakhand in january, but at that time i never thought we would become this close. i still remember jim corbett when you came and asked about the gypsy seat allotments. then we met again in rishikesh after the ganga pooja. we barely knew each other, and somehow after the trip we started talking on instagram because of my phone's stupid ghost touch issue.

genuinely, who would've thought that something as random as that would end up with us talking this much?

at first it was just normal conversations. then slowly we started talking more and more. random things, stupid jokes, serious stuff sometimes, literally everything. and i don't even know when it happened, but you just became someone i'm really comfortable with.

i can just say whatever stupid thing comes to my mind, irritate you, joke around, and then randomly have a proper conversation with you five minutes later. somewhere in between all this, you became someone i genuinely care about.

03 · you, just as you are

it's not just how
you make me feel. it's you.

i think what makes you special to me is something beyond just the way you make me feel. it's the person you are and all the little things that make you who you are. i love how there are so many different sides to you, the way you can be playful one moment and thoughtful the next, the way you express yourself, the little things you get excited about, and even the things you sometimes overthink.

04 · what love means to me

more than
three words.

i know we haven't really said those three words to each other yet, and honestly, i don't want to rush into saying them just because they're expected at some point. sometimes there are things you feel long before you know how to put them into words. with you, i think i'm slowly getting there.

for me, love has never really been just about saying those three words. it's being there when things get difficult. it's trusting each other, putting in effort, listening, remembering the little things, making time, caring, and choosing to stay. it's making each other feel safe and understood. it's showing up without being asked and reminding each other that we don't have to face everything alone.

i don't just want to tell you that i love you someday. i want you to feel it in the way i treat you, the way i show up for you, the way i care about you, and the way i stand beside you when you need me. and if someday i look at you and say those three words, i want them to mean: i choose you, i trust you, i care about you, and i'll keep putting in the effort to be someone you can count on.

not rushed. not borrowed. just real.

keep working towards your dreams, discover yourself, grow, make mistakes, learn new things, and never feel that you have to slow yourself down for me. i'd rather see you becoming everything you've ever wanted to be than have you feel like you need to compromise on yourself.

because i don't want to be just a temporary happy memory in your life. i'd love to be someone who becomes a meaningful part of your journey.
